# Project Panel Planning

This document plans future project panels for the current portfolio system without creating the panels yet.

The goal is to stay faithful to the current portfolio language:
- ambitious, premium, visually led
- strong problem framing
- research + product + systems thinking
- health, policy, AI, equity, and venture-building as the recurring threads

This plan draws on the current portfolio repo, the existing SWAAD/NOURISH direction, the public CV/profile information already reviewed, and the broader shape of Stefan Thottunkal's work:
- digital health and precision medicine
- AI in healthcare
- Indigenous and community health implementation
- public health and health systems
- venture building and startup design
- conference and research outputs that can be translated into interactive digital case studies

## Guardrails

Before adding new panels, keep these rules:
- Only add panels where there is a real story, not just a topic.
- Prefer projects with a clear challenge, intervention, and outcome trajectory.
- Mix project types: venture, research translation, public health, platform, policy, and experimental builds.
- Avoid generic "student portfolio filler" projects.
- Favor projects that show Stefan as a builder, strategist, researcher, or designer, not just a participant.
- Each panel should justify a visually rich, interactive experience in the existing system.
